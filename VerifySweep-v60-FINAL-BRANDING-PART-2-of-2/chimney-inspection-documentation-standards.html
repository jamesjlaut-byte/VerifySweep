Documenting Chimney Inspection Findings Without Overstating Them

Better reports separate observation, source, interpretation and recommendation.

VerifySweep field rule: Identify the system, document the condition, identify the source, confirm the edition/jurisdiction, and do not claim more than the evidence supports.

Start with what you actually observed. Photographs, measurements, labels and accessible conditions are stronger than conclusions without supporting documentation.

State limitations. Concealed areas, inaccessible flues, weather, roof access, missing labels and scope restrictions can materially affect what an inspection can establish.

Use precise language: observed, could not verify, appears consistent with, requires further evaluation. Reserve definitive conclusions for conditions the evidence supports.

GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE EDUCATION

Google Business Profile red flags: what deserves a closer look?

Google Business Profiles are useful discovery tools, but a profile is not a professional credential. VerifySweep encourages users to verify the business identity, the person performing the work, and claims that can be independently checked.

Signals worth verifying

Business identity: Does the public-facing name match the company’s real-world identity? Google says businesses should be represented as they are consistently recognized in the real world.

Location: Does the address make sense for the claimed operation? A questionable address can justify additional verification, but it does not by itself establish wrongdoing.

Routing: Does the listed phone or website consistently identify the same company, or does it appear to route to a different identity?

Multiple listings: Shared phones, domains, addresses or repeated wording can be useful relationship leads. Relationships must still be verified.

Lead generation: Google currently lists lead-generation agents or companies among examples of businesses that are not eligible for a Business Profile.

What not to do

Do not assume a competitor is illegitimate because it ranks highly, has many reviews, uses subcontractors or operates across a large service area. Document what can actually be checked.

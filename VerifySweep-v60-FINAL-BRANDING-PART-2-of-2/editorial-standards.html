TRUST & TRANSPARENCY

How VerifySweep decides what can be stated as fact.

VerifySweep is built around a simple rule: a marketing claim, allegation, shared phone number or AI-generated theory does not become a verified fact simply because it appears plausible.

1 · PRIMARY SOURCES

Verify at the source.

Credential claims should be checked against the issuing organization when an official public verification source is available. Google policy claims should be checked against current official Google Business Profile policy pages rather than screenshots or secondhand summaries.

2 · INDIVIDUAL VS COMPANY

Attribute credentials correctly.

When a credential belongs to an individual, VerifySweep treats it as an individual credential. A company displaying a credential logo does not automatically establish that every employee holds that credential.

3 · EVIDENCE LABELS

Say what is known—and what is not.

Evidence may be labeled unreviewed, reporter-observed, independently verified or disputed. Relationship indicators remain leads until supported by evidence that justifies a stronger conclusion.

4 · AI LIMITS

AI assists. Humans review.

AI may organize information, identify questions and compare supported facts with policy text. It does not independently declare a company fraudulent, determine ownership from a shared identifier or make Google enforcement decisions.

5 · CORRECTIONS

Evidence must remain correctable.

Material factual errors should be corrected when reliable information supports a change. Disagreement and factual correction are treated differently, and disputed information should remain labeled rather than silently converted into fact.

6 · SOURCE DATES

Policies and directories change.

VerifySweep records or displays source-review dates where practical. A previously verified credential or policy statement may need to be checked again before a homeowner makes a hiring decision or a professional prepares a current complaint.

Independence statement: VerifySweep is an independent information and investigation platform. Listing or linking to CSIA, NFI, F.I.R.E., NCSG, Google or another organization does not imply sponsorship, endorsement or affiliation unless explicitly stated.

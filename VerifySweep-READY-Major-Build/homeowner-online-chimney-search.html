How to Research a Chimney Company Online

Google is a discovery tool. This guide shows homeowners how to move from a search result to independent verification.

1. Use search to build a shortlist

Ranking, ads and review counts can help you discover companies, but they do not independently establish training or workmanship.

2. Identify the real company

Compare the company name, website, phone, service area and people represented as technicians.

3. Verify credential claims

Check named professionals with the issuing organization whenever an official verification resource is available.

4. Read reviews for substance

Look for detailed experiences over time rather than treating a star average as a credential.

5. Slow down on major repairs

When a recommendation is expensive, invasive or difficult to reverse, consider getting clear documentation and another qualified opinion.

VerifySweep standard: Evidence should support the exact statement being made. A suspicious indicator is a reason to investigate, not a verdict.

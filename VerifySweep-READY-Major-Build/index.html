WHY CREDENTIALS MATTER

A Google search can help you find a company. Credentials help you investigate who you are hiring.

A polished website, prominent search position or large number of reviews does not by itself establish a technician’s chimney or hearth training. One of the most useful screening steps a homeowner can take is to verify the credentials held by the individual professional who will perform or supervise the work.

Recognized professional certification

CSIA offers chimney and venting credentials including Certified Chimney Sweep, with advanced continuing-education designations. NFI certifies specialists in woodburning, gas, pellet and hearth design. F.I.R.E. provides specialized inspection education and credentialing. NCSG provides industry education and currently lists Certified Chimney Professional credentials.

Verify the person — not just the logo

Certification, guild membership, manufacturer training and field experience are different things. VerifySweep is being built to show the named credential holder, credential type, issuing organization and verification source so homeowners can make a better-informed decision.

PROFESSIONAL INVESTIGATION PROCESS

AI helps us investigate. Human review controls the conclusion.

VerifySweep uses AI-assisted tools to triage reports, find possible connections, organize evidence, compare verified facts with policy, and prepare factual material for review. No single tool gets to decide that a company is fraudulent.

What happens after a professional brings us a concern

The goal is not to automate accusations. The goal is to help chimney professionals turn scattered observations into a documented investigation that can be reviewed carefully and, when appropriate, prepared for Google.

FraudWatch AI™

Finds things worth checking. Reviews a submission for possible patterns, duplicate information, inconsistencies and missing evidence. A flag is a question, not a finding of fraud.

Integrity Graph™

Looks for connections. Organizes relationships among phone numbers, domains, addresses, listings and prior cases so a reviewer can see whether supposedly separate entities may share infrastructure.

Evidence AI

Organizes the proof. Separates screenshots, URLs, documents and reporter statements from information VerifySweep independently confirms.

CaseBuilder AI™

Builds the timeline. Turns scattered evidence into a readable chronology showing what was reported, what was checked and what remains unknown.

PolicyCheck AI™

Compares facts with policy. Identifies Google rules that may be relevant to verified facts. Google—not VerifySweep—makes Google enforcement decisions.

ReportBuilder AI™

Prepares a factual report. Converts reviewed evidence into a concise package for the appropriate official reporting channel.

ChangeWatch AI™

Tracks what changes. Helps record meaningful changes such as names, websites, phone numbers, addresses, removals or reappearances when those changes can be observed.

Human Review

The required judgment step. Evidence, context and uncertainty are reviewed before a conclusion is presented as verified.

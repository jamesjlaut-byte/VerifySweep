CREDENTIAL COMPARISON

There is no single credential that means “knows everything.”

Chimney and hearth credentials cover different bodies of knowledge. VerifySweep compares them by scope instead of turning education into a popularity contest.

Verify the individual. A logo on a truck or website is not enough. Ask which person holds the credential, whether it is current, and whether that person will perform or supervise the work.

How VerifySweep will display credentials

Directory profiles should show the named credential holder, credential or education type, issuing organization, verification source and status. Memberships and manufacturer training should be labeled separately rather than blended together as “certifications.”

Reporting Competitors Responsibly: Evidence Before Accusations

A reporting system only helps the chimney industry if legitimate competition is separated from supportable policy concerns.

Competition is not evidence.

Lower prices, aggressive advertising, high rankings, or operating in the same service area are not reasons by themselves to report a business.

Document the exact concern.

Preserve the listing URL, business name, phone, website, displayed location, screenshots, dates, and the specific fact that raised the concern. Keep what you verified separate from what you suspect.

Relationships are leads, not verdicts.

A shared phone, domain, address, person, or wording can justify additional research. It does not automatically establish common ownership, fraud, or a policy violation.

WHAT VERIFICATION MEANS

No blanket “certified company” label.

VerifySweep separates business identity from individual credentials. A result should show who holds the credential, what the credential is, who issued it, where it was checked and when it was checked.

VERIFY AT THE SOURCE

Check the credential with the organization that issued it.

Do not rely only on a logo displayed on a company website. Verify the named professional whenever an official directory is available.

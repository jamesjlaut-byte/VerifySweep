VerifySweep is designed to investigate evidence, not punish businesses through accusation. Any business or professional referenced in a VerifySweep case should have a practical path to challenge inaccurate information.

What to provide: identify the disputed statement or data point, explain why it is inaccurate, and provide supporting material that can be independently checked.

Dispute & Correction Process

What happens next

VerifySweep reviews the disputed item against the original source and the new evidence. If a correction is supported, the record should be corrected and the material change logged. If evidence remains conflicting, the record should say so rather than presenting uncertainty as fact.

What this process is not

A dispute request does not require VerifySweep to remove accurate source material merely because it is unfavorable, and a report from a competitor does not by itself establish wrongdoing.

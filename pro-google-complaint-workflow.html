FOR CHIMNEY PROFESSIONALS

Turn a field observation into a documented, reviewable complaint package.

A step-by-step professional workflow for investigating potential lead generators and misleading listings.

Stage 1 — Triage

Record what triggered the concern and identify which claims can actually be checked.

Stage 2 — Relationship mapping

Compare phone numbers, domains, websites, addresses, names and related listings for potentially meaningful connections.

Stage 3 — Evidence control

Preserve source URLs, screenshots, dates and verified observations. Keep suspicions separately labeled.

Stage 4 — Case assembly

Create a concise timeline and identify evidence gaps before making a policy claim.

Stage 5 — Policy comparison

Compare supported facts with current Google Business Profile rules. Google currently lists lead-generation agents or companies among ineligible examples.

Stage 6 — Complaint preparation

Prepare a concise package for the appropriate Google reporting route. VerifySweep assists; Google makes the enforcement decision.

Evidence standard: Preserve what you observed, identify the source, distinguish inference from fact, and use current official Google policy when preparing a complaint.

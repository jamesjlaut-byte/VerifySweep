YOUR VERIFICATION PATH

Four checks before you hire.

VerifySweep is built around the individual professional—not just the company name or a certification logo shown in advertising.

01 · IDENTIFY

Get the technician’s name

Ask which individual will inspect, sweep, repair, or advise on your chimney.
02 · VERIFY

Check the claimed credential

Use the credential issuer’s official source whenever public verification is available.
03 · UNDERSTAND

Know what it means

Different credentials represent different education, testing, specialties, and requirements.
04 · DECIDE

Use the hiring checklist

Credentials are one part of the decision. Review the company, scope of work, documentation, and questions before approving expensive repairs.

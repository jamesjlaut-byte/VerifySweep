ABOUT VERIFYSWEEP

Transparency without turning suspicion into a verdict.

VerifySweep is being built to help homeowners verify who they are hiring and to give chimney professionals a factual way to document suspicious business patterns.

Two audiences. One standard: follow the evidence.

HOMEOWNERS

Make hiring decisions with clearer information.

VerifySweep separates business identity, individual credentials, verification sources and review findings instead of treating an advertised badge as proof of everything.

Homeowner tools
CHIMNEY PROFESSIONALS

Report what you found without having to prove the entire case.

A professional can submit evidence or a concern. VerifySweep then separates what was reported, what can be independently verified, what remains uncertain and what requires human review.

Professional workspace
VerifySweep does not determine that a business is fraudulent simply because someone reports it. Reports are leads. Indicators are not verdicts. Enforcement decisions belong to the relevant platform or authority.
EDITORIAL PRINCIPLE

Credentials matter. Education still continues.

VerifySweep supports legitimate certification and professional education without turning the industry into competing certificate teams. Credentials establish a foundation. Education builds on it. Experience tests it. Continuing education keeps it relevant.

HOW VERIFYSWEEP BUILDS TRUST

Official sources. Clear evidence labels. Human review.

Credential claims are checked at the issuing source when possible. Investigation findings keep verified facts separate from allegations and AI-assisted leads.

Verify who is actually coming to your home

A company may advertise credentials held by an owner or another employee. Homeowners should understand whether the person performing or supervising their work holds the advertised qualification.

A company credential claim and an individual credential are not always the same thing.

Step 1 — Ask for the technician's name

If a company advertises certification, ask whether the scheduled technician personally holds it or whether a credentialed person will supervise the work.

Step 2 — Ask for the exact credential

Write down the credential name and issuer rather than relying on the word “certified.”

Step 3 — Verify through the issuer

CSIA credential directory · NFI Find a Pro · F.I.R.E. official website · NCSG official website

Step 4 — Ask about relevant experience

Certification is valuable evidence of education, but system-specific experience, manufacturer training and continuing education can matter too.

HOW RESULTS ARE LABELED

A credential belongs to a person—not automatically to every employee of a company.

VerifySweep will show who holds the credential and where it was verified. We do not use a blanket “certified company” label when the underlying credential is issued to an individual.

DIRECTORY BUILD

VerifySweep search only shows records that have passed verification.

Search results come from VerifySweep's own reviewed directory and retain a link to the credential source. Pending or unverified submissions are excluded from homeowner results. When an area has not yet been indexed, use the official CSIA and NFI directories rather than relying on guessed results.

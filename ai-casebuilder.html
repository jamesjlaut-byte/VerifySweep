Step 4 — Build the factual case

CaseBuilder AI

Turn evidence into a readable timeline that shows what happened, what was verified and what remains unknown.

OUTPUT
Case timeline

What it does

CaseBuilder AI assembles the investigation into a clear chronology. It connects the original concern, the evidence collected, relationship findings, credential checks, listing changes and unresolved questions.

Why chronology matters

A complaint is easier to evaluate when a reviewer can follow the sequence. Instead of dumping screenshots into a form, the case explains what each item proves and how it relates to the issue being reported.

Keep the case narrow

CaseBuilder should avoid irrelevant accusations. The strongest case focuses on the specific business identity or policy issue supported by evidence and leaves unrelated suspicions out.

Show gaps openly

If a key fact is missing, the case should say so. A transparent gap is more credible than an unsupported conclusion.

What comes next

PolicyCheck AI compares the verified facts in the case with the current Google Business Profile rules that may apply.

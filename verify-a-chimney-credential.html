HOMEOWNER GUIDE

How to verify a chimney credential instead of trusting a logo.

Verification should answer four questions: Who holds it? What is it? Who issued it? Is it current?

CSIA

CSIA currently lists Certified Chimney Sweep, Certified Chimney Specialist and Master Chimney Sweep credentials, among others. Its public directory is specifically intended to help locate professionals and verify credentials.

Verify with CSIA

NFI

NFI certifies Gas, Woodburning, Pellet and Hearth Design Specialists. Individuals holding all three fuel-specific certifications are recognized as Master Hearth Professionals.

Verify with NFI

F.I.R.E.

For a claimed F.I.R.E. credential, use the organization's own resources and verify the individual rather than assuming a company-wide credential.

Visit F.I.R.E.

NCSG / CCP

NCSG's directory distinguishes member status and certifications. Verify the specific certification shown for the individual.

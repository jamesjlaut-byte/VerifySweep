Step 6 — Prepare the Google complaint

ReportBuilder AI

Convert the reviewed case into a concise, evidence-based package that is easier for Google to evaluate.

OUTPUT
Google complaint package

What it does

ReportBuilder AI summarizes the strongest verified facts, source links, screenshots, relationships, timeline and relevant policy references into a complaint-ready format.

Facts first

The complaint should describe what can be shown, not what the reporter assumes. Avoid emotional language, competitor attacks and unsupported labels such as scam or fraud when the evidence does not establish them.

Use the right reporting route

Different Google problems may require different reporting or review paths. VerifySweep should help identify the appropriate official channel rather than treating every issue as the same complaint.

Human review before filing

A professional should review names, URLs, evidence descriptions and policy references before submitting anything. AI can organize and draft; the person filing remains responsible for accuracy.

Google makes the decision

VerifySweep helps prepare the complaint. It does not submit a verdict and cannot promise a removal. Google independently evaluates the report and determines what action, if any, to take.

THE VERIFY SWEEP STANDARD

AI assists. Evidence leads. Human review controls the conclusion.

Every stage is designed to make a chimney-industry concern easier to investigate without turning suspicion into an unsupported accusation.

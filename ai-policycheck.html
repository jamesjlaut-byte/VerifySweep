Step 5 — Compare facts with Google policy

PolicyCheck AI

Match verified evidence to the Google Business Profile rules that may actually be relevant.

OUTPUT
Policy analysis

What it does

PolicyCheck AI reviews the verified case facts and identifies Google Business Profile policies that may be relevant, such as eligibility, business representation, address and service-area rules, or other listing requirements.

Policy is not a verdict

VerifySweep can explain why a fact may relate to a Google policy, but it does not decide whether Google will remove, suspend or change a profile. Google controls its own enforcement.

Use current official sources

Policy references should come from current official Google support pages whenever possible. Old screenshots, forum posts and third-party summaries can become outdated.

Evidence must support the policy concern

A policy citation does not strengthen a weak factual case by itself. The verified evidence should explain exactly what was observed and why that observation may conflict with the cited rule.

What comes next

ReportBuilder AI converts the reviewed evidence and relevant policy references into a concise complaint package for the appropriate Google reporting channel.

How to read a chimney repair estimate

A good chimney estimate should tell you what was found, why work is recommended, what will be installed or repaired, and what evidence supports the recommendation.

What to look for

Look for a specific scope of work rather than broad phrases such as “repair chimney.” Materials, affected areas, access requirements and major components should be identifiable.

What to document

The estimate should connect the proposed work to inspection findings. Photographs, measurements and clear descriptions make it easier to understand the recommendation and compare a second opinion.

How to use it

Credentials do not guarantee a correct estimate, but verifying the professional’s relevant training is an important screening step—especially when the proposed work involves venting, liners, hearth appliances or major structural repair.

Independent sources

Credential and platform-policy claims should be checked against the issuing organization or platform. VerifySweep encourages direct verification rather than relying on a logo, advertisement or third-party claim.

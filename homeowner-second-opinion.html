When should you get a second opinion on chimney repairs?

A recommendation for major chimney work can involve safety, substantial cost and parts of the system that homeowners cannot easily see. Asking for documentation or another qualified opinion is reasonable.

Consider another opinion when

• The recommended repair is expensive or involves major reconstruction, relining or replacement.

• The explanation relies on fear but provides little photographic or written documentation.

• You are told a system is unsafe but are not shown the observed condition or the basis for that conclusion.

• The person cannot clearly identify the fireplace/chimney system, applicable manufacturer instructions, or the defect being discussed.

• A company pressures you to authorize major work immediately when there is no immediate emergency.

What a useful second opinion should do

Ask the second professional to inspect independently rather than simply agree or disagree with the first estimate. Give them access to prior reports after they have documented their own observations when practical.

Credentials are part of the screening process

Check the actual technician’s relevant credentials and experience. Credentials do not guarantee the conclusion, but independent verification gives you more information than reviews or advertising alone.

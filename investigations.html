WHAT THIS TOOL IS FOR

From suspicious listing to a reviewable Google complaint package.

A sweep may encounter a company that appears to use false locations, conflicting names, reused phone numbers, questionable credential claims, or a lead-generation business model. VerifySweep provides a disciplined process to identify the exact Google profile, preserve public evidence, test the concern against reliable sources and current Google policy, and draft neutral complaint language. The professional or authorized reviewer still verifies the evidence and files through Google; VerifySweep does not automatically accuse the business or submit the complaint.

1. IDENTIFYRecord the exact Google profile and business being reviewed.
2. PRESERVESave URLs, screenshots, dates, names, phones and addresses.
3. CONNECTDocument possible relationships without assuming ownership.
4. VERIFYSeparate confirmed facts from claims and suspicions.
5. CHECK POLICYCompare supported facts with current Google rules.
6. DRAFTPrepare concise complaint language and an evidence index.
7. HUMAN FILESReview every statement and use the appropriate Google channel.

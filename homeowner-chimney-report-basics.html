What a Useful Chimney Inspection Report Should Show

A practical homeowner guide to documentation that makes chimney inspection findings easier to understand and evaluate.

1. Identify the system

The report should make clear what fireplace, chimney or venting system was evaluated.

2. Show important findings

Photos and plain-language descriptions help connect recommendations to observable conditions.

3. Separate observation from recommendation

A report should distinguish what was found from what the contractor proposes doing about it.

4. Explain major limitations

Areas that could not be accessed or evaluated should not disappear from the report.

5. Make major repairs understandable

For expensive work, the homeowner should be able to understand the condition, proposed correction and why it was recommended.

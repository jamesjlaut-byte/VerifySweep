HOMEOWNER GUIDE

Before you hire a chimney company: verify more than the reviews.

Use Google to discover companies, then independently check the person, credentials, business identity and scope of work before you decide who enters your home.

Quick rule: A high Google ranking is advertising visibility—not proof of chimney training, certification, insurance, workmanship or business identity.

10 checks before hiring

Identify the actual company. Confirm the legal/public business name, website, phone number and who will perform the work.
Ask who holds the credential. A logo on a website is not the same as verifying the technician or supervisor.
Verify CSIA credentials. Use the issuer's public directory rather than relying only on a badge. Open CSIA verification →
Verify NFI credentials when hearth/appliance expertise matters. NFI offers fuel-specific specialist certifications. Find an NFI Pro →
Check inspection-specific education. If someone claims F.I.R.E. certification, verify the individual with the issuer. Visit F.I.R.E. →
Separate membership from certification. Association membership can be valuable, but it should not be represented as a technical certification unless the person separately holds one.
Ask for a written scope. Inspection level, proposed repair, materials, exclusions and price should be understandable before major work begins.
Check insurance and business information. For substantial repair work, ask for current insurance information and make sure the company identity matches the proposal.
Be cautious with urgency. Serious defects can exist, but pressure should not replace documentation. Ask to see photos, video or other evidence supporting expensive recommendations.
Get another qualified opinion when the stakes are high. Relines, rebuilds and major fireplace repairs can justify a second opinion.

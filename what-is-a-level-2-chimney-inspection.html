What is a Level 2 chimney inspection?

A practical homeowner guide to understanding when a more detailed chimney inspection may be appropriate, what documentation to expect, and why the inspector’s qualifications matter.

What to look for

When homeowners hear “Level 2,” the important point is not the label alone. The inspection scope should match the circumstances of the property and fireplace system, and the findings should be documented clearly.

What to document

Ask who is performing the inspection, what chimney or hearth credentials they personally hold, what areas will be examined, whether accessible concealed areas are included where applicable, and what photographs or written findings you will receive.

How to use it

A recommendation for expensive repair should be traceable to an observed condition. If the report is vague, the evidence is weak, or the proposed repair is substantial, consider an independent second opinion before authorizing major work.

Independent sources

Step 3 — Organize the evidence

Evidence AI

Separate what is verified from what is alleged, inferred or still missing.

OUTPUT
Verified evidence file

What it does

Evidence AI turns scattered screenshots, URLs, notes, listings, records and observations into a structured evidence file. Each item should identify its source, date, what it actually shows and whether it has been independently checked.

Verified facts vs. allegations

Reporter statements are kept separate from independently supportable facts. If a sweep says two businesses are connected, that statement is an allegation until the underlying identifiers or records support it.

Evidence strength

Direct source material generally carries more weight than assumptions: official listings, captured webpages, public records, credential directories, documented redirects and dated screenshots are more useful than unsupported conclusions.

Preserve uncertainty

Good evidence files include contradictions and unknowns. If something cannot be confirmed, Evidence AI should label it unresolved rather than force it into the theory of the case.

What comes next

CaseBuilder AI uses the organized evidence to create a chronology and case narrative without blurring the line between fact and inference.

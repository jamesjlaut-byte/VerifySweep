HOMEOWNER GUIDE

Verify before you hire

A five-minute credential and identity check can give you information that rankings, ads and star ratings cannot.

A simple VerifySweep hiring sequence

1 — Find

Use search engines, referrals and professional directories to build a shortlist.

2 — Identify

Find out who will perform or supervise the chimney or hearth work.

3 — Verify

Check claimed industry credentials with the organization that issued them.

4 — Understand

Ask what the inspection or repair includes and request understandable documentation.

5 — Decide

Consider credentials alongside experience, insurance, documentation, communication and the specific work being proposed.

Credentials are not a guarantee. They are one of the strongest independently checkable pieces of information available to a homeowner before hiring.

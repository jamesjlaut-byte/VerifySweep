IRC Chapter 10: Chimneys & Fireplaces for Working Sweeps

A practical map of where chimney professionals encounter the International Residential Code and why Chapter 10 matters.

VerifySweep field rule: Identify the system, document the condition, identify the source, confirm the edition/jurisdiction, and do not claim more than the evidence supports.

The 2024 IRC places chimneys and fireplaces in Chapter 10. Its organization includes masonry fireplaces, masonry heaters, masonry chimneys, factory-built fireplaces, factory-built chimneys and exterior air supply.

For sweeps, the distinction between masonry and factory-built systems is critical. Prescriptive masonry provisions and listed factory-built systems are not interchangeable approaches. With listed systems, identification, listing, labeling and manufacturer installation instructions can be central to evaluating the installation.

Do not assume the newest model IRC is the code enforced at a particular property. Determine the adopted edition, local amendments and authority having jurisdiction when a code-compliance conclusion matters.

Certification Isn’t the Finish Line

A credential demonstrates that someone reached a standard. It should never convince a professional that there is nothing left to learn. Codes, standards, manufacturer instructions, field experience, continuing education and knowing when to ask for help all matter.

VerifySweep principle

Verify credentials. Respect legitimate education. Follow the evidence. Keep learning. Protect the homeowner.

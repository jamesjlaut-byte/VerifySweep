TERMS & USE

Verify facts. Do not weaponize suspicion.

Information, not a fraud verdict

VerifySweep organizes information, credentials, observations and evidence. A report, indicator, relationship lead or AI-assisted analysis is not by itself a determination that a person or business committed fraud, violated law, or violated a platform policy.

Responsible reporting

Users should submit information they reasonably believe is accurate, distinguish firsthand observations from theories, preserve source material, and correct material errors when discovered. VerifySweep should not be used for harassment, retaliation, fabricated complaints or coordinated attacks on competitors.

Credentials

Credential information is educational and should be independently verified with the issuing organization. Certification, membership, manufacturer training and field experience are different categories. No credential guarantees workmanship.

Platform decisions

Google and other platforms make their own eligibility and enforcement decisions. VerifySweep can help organize evidence and prepare a report; it does not control the outcome.

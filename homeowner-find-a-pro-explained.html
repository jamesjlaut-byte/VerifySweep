How VerifySweep Find a Pro Works

What homeowners should understand about VerifySweep directory results and credential verification.

1. Directory results are a starting point

A listing helps identify a professional and the credential information VerifySweep has recorded.

2. Verification should point to the issuer

Where possible, credential claims should connect back to the organization that issued them.

3. No directory can guarantee workmanship

Credentials and verification add useful information, but homeowners still need to evaluate scope, documentation, insurance and fit for the job.

4. Verify the person

Pay attention to the named credential holder and ask who will actually perform or supervise the work.

5. Use independent judgment

For major repairs, combine credentials with clear inspection evidence and an understandable written proposal.

VerifySweep standard: Evidence should support the exact statement being made. A suspicious indicator is a reason to investigate, not a verdict.

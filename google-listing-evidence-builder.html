Google Listing Evidence Builder

Record exactly what a public listing shows, preserve its source, and separate observed facts from possible conflicts.

Listing evidence

This tool does not contact Google or decide that a listing violates policy. It prepares a structured record for review.

Evidence standard: A possible inconsistency is not proof of fraud, misconduct, or wrongdoing. Preserve screenshots separately and verify current Google policy before submitting a complaint.
VerifySweep organizes public evidence for review. Google makes its own enforcement decisions.

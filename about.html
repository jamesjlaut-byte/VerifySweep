ABOUT VERIFYSWEEP

Transparency without turning suspicion into a verdict.

VerifySweep exists to help homeowners and chimney professionals separate verifiable facts from marketing claims, document concerning business patterns, and follow evidence back to authoritative sources.

Two audiences. One standard: follow the evidence.

VerifySweep serves homeowners who want to know who they are hiring and legitimate chimney professionals who want a fair, organized way to document questionable marketplace activity.

For Homeowners

A company logo, online review profile, certification badge, or polished website does not by itself verify the individual who will arrive at your home.

VerifySweep helps homeowners identify the chimney professional, understand recognized industry credentials, and verify claimed credentials through official sources whenever possible.

For Chimney Professionals

Legitimate professionals can encounter lead generators, misleading business identities, copied credentials, questionable listings, or other patterns that deserve closer review.

VerifySweep helps organize evidence, investigate connections, and prepare documentation that may assist with reports submitted to platforms such as Google.

PROFESSIONAL RESOURCES
CREDENTIALS MATTER

Verify the individual — not just the company.

Recognized chimney-industry credentials generally belong to individual professionals. Seeing a certification logo in advertising does not automatically establish that the person coming to your home currently holds that credential.

VERIFY BEFORE YOU HIRE.

When possible, VerifySweep directs users back to the credential issuer or another authoritative source so the information can be checked independently.

HOW WE APPROACH INVESTIGATIONS

Evidence first.

VerifySweep does not treat a complaint, unusual pattern, or suspicion as proof of wrongdoing.

  • Document the claim or concern.
  • Preserve relevant public evidence.
  • Identify connections that can actually be supported.
  • Separate confirmed facts from unresolved questions.
  • Use authoritative sources whenever available.
  • Prepare organized evidence for appropriate review channels.

The goal is not to manufacture accusations. The goal is to make verification and documentation easier.

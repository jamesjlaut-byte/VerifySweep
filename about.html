ABOUT VERIFYSWEEP

Transparency without turning suspicion into a verdict.

VerifySweep is a verification and research platform for the chimney and hearth industry. It helps homeowners make more informed hiring decisions and gives legitimate professionals practical tools to document marketplace concerns, organize evidence, and follow claims back to authoritative sources.

Two audiences. One standard: follow the evidence.

VerifySweep serves homeowners who want to know who they are hiring and chimney professionals who want a fair, organized process for protecting customers, their businesses, and the integrity of the industry.

For Homeowners

A company logo, online review profile, certification badge, or polished website does not by itself verify the individual who will arrive at your home.

VerifySweep brings professional discovery, credential education, official verification links, and ChimneyAI homeowner guidance into one place. It encourages homeowners to ask who will actually perform the work and verify that individual before approving an inspection or costly repair.

HOW WE APPROACH INVESTIGATIONS

Evidence first.

VerifySweep does not treat a complaint, unusual pattern, or suspicion as proof of wrongdoing.

  • Document the claim or concern.
  • Preserve relevant public evidence.
  • Identify connections that can actually be supported.
  • Separate confirmed facts from unresolved questions.
  • Use authoritative sources whenever available.
  • Prepare organized evidence for appropriate review channels.

The goal is not to manufacture accusations. The goal is to make verification and documentation easier, while clearly separating verified facts, unverified claims, possible concerns, and questions that still need evidence.

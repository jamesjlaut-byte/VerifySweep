PROFESSIONAL INVESTIGATION STANDARD

Investigating Questionable Business Names on Google

How chimney professionals can document potentially misleading Business Profile names using real-world branding evidence.

1. Capture the profile name

Record the exact displayed name and listing URL.

2. Find real-world identity signals

Compare the name with the company website, state records, vehicles, signage and other authoritative business materials where available.

3. Avoid keyword assumptions

A keyword-rich name can be a lead to investigate, but the name alone does not establish a violation.

4. Record contradictions

Document specific differences between the profile and independently verifiable real-world branding.

5. Prepare a factual summary

State what each source shows and let Google determine whether its representation rules are met.

VerifySweep standard: Evidence should support the exact statement being made. A suspicious indicator is a reason to investigate, not a verdict.

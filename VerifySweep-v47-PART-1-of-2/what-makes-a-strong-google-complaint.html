PROFESSIONAL GUIDE

What makes a strong Google Business Profile complaint?

A useful complaint is specific, sourced, reproducible and tied to the exact profile or representation being challenged.

Build evidence another reviewer can check.

Exact target

Preserve the Business Profile/Maps URL, displayed name, phone, website and location information.

Observed facts

State what you personally verified and how you verified it. Keep theories in a separate section.

Source material

Save URLs, dated screenshots, public records and credential-directory results where relevant.

Relationships

Shared phones, domains, addresses or wording can justify further review, but a relationship alone does not prove common ownership or wrongdoing.

Policy relevance

Google says Business Profiles generally require in-person customer contact and lists lead-generation agents or companies among examples of ineligible businesses.

Concise explanation

Explain why the verified facts may matter under the relevant policy without exaggerating what the evidence establishes.

VerifySweep principle: document first, characterize second. Google makes the enforcement decision.

Step 6 — Prepare the Google complaint

ReportBuilder AI

Build a structured evidence package for human review, including verified observations, sources, relationships, chronology, policy references, unresolved questions and a factual complaint-support draft.

OUTPUT
Human-reviewed evidence package

What it builds

ReportBuilder assembles the connected case into a reviewable package: case overview, independently verified observations, evidence inventory, relationship leads, chronology, official policy sources, unresolved questions, filing-readiness gates and the complaint-support draft.

Facts first

The complaint should describe what can be shown, not what the reporter assumes. Avoid emotional language, competitor attacks and unsupported labels such as scam or fraud when the evidence does not establish them.

Use the right reporting route

Different Google problems may require different reporting or review paths. VerifySweep should help identify the appropriate official channel rather than treating every issue as the same complaint.

Review, export, then decide

The package can be printed or saved as a PDF for review and exported as structured case data. Filing controls stay gated until the required human review checks are complete. The person filing remains responsible for accuracy.

Google makes the decision

VerifySweep helps prepare the complaint. It does not submit a verdict and cannot promise a removal. Google independently evaluates the report and determines what action, if any, to take.

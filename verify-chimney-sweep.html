How to verify a chimney sweep

The key is connecting the company’s claim to the individual who will actually inspect, sweep, install, or repair your chimney.

1Ask who is coming.Get the technician’s full name before the appointment when possible.
2Ask what they hold.Get the exact credential and issuing organization—not just “we’re certified.”
3Check the official source.Search for the individual and confirm the credential appears current.
4Match the business.Confirm the company, phone, website, and person you were told would arrive.

Credentials matter—but they are not a guarantee.
Verification helps confirm that a named person appears to hold a claimed credential. It does not guarantee workmanship, honesty, code compliance, or the outcome of a job.

If a company will not tell you who is coming to your home, cannot identify who actually holds the credential it advertises, or gives you information that does not match across its website, phone number, and business listing, verify more carefully before authorizing work.

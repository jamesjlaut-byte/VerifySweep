THE VERIFYSWEEP AI INVESTIGATION SYSTEM

Six focused AI stages turn a suspicious listing into evidence you can actually use.

Each stage has one job. The system starts by asking what looks unusual, then checks for relationships, organizes the source material, builds the case, compares verified facts with Google policy, and prepares the strongest supportable report. AI assists at every stage, but a human remains responsible for the conclusions.

01 — FIND WHAT NEEDS CHECKING

FraudWatch AI

The first-pass investigator. FraudWatch reviews the company, Google listing and evidence submitted by the reporter for indicators that deserve verification. It can flag conflicting business information, questionable locations, duplicate or unusually similar listings, reused contact information, unverifiable credential claims and other inconsistencies. A flag means “investigate this,” not “this company is fraudulent.”

02 — FIND THE CONNECTIONS

Integrity Graph

The relationship mapper. Integrity Graph checks whether apparently separate companies or listings may be connected through shared phone numbers, websites, domains, addresses, people, business names or previously documented cases. It helps reveal patterns that are difficult to see one listing at a time. A relationship is recorded according to the evidence supporting it and is not automatically treated as common ownership or wrongdoing.

03 — CONTROL THE EVIDENCE

Evidence AI

The evidence organizer. Evidence AI turns scattered screenshots, URLs, directory results, dates, ads, business records and reporter observations into a structured evidence file. It keeps what the reporter alleges separate from what VerifySweep can independently verify, identifies missing support, and preserves the source behind important facts so another reviewer can retrace the investigation.

04 — BUILD THE CASE

CaseBuilder AI

The case assembler. CaseBuilder converts the organized evidence into a readable investigation: who or what was reported, what was observed, what was independently verified, what relationships were found, what remains uncertain and when key events occurred. The result is a factual case timeline instead of a pile of screenshots or an unsupported accusation.

05 — CHECK GOOGLE'S RULES

PolicyCheck AI

The policy comparison stage. PolicyCheck compares the facts that survived verification with the Google Business Profile rules relevant to the case—such as business eligibility, representation, locations, service-area businesses and lead-generation concerns. It identifies which policy language may matter and which evidence supports the concern. It does not decide that Google must remove a listing; Google makes that enforcement decision.

06 — PREPARE THE GOOGLE REPORT

ReportBuilder AI

The reporting assistant. ReportBuilder takes the strongest verified evidence and turns it into a concise, source-backed package that explains the listing, the potential policy issue, the supporting facts, relevant connections and attached evidence. The goal is to help a chimney professional submit a clearer, better-supported report through the appropriate Google reporting or review process—not simply send Google an accusation.

HOW VERIFYSWEEP HELPS PROFESSIONALS

You find something suspicious. VerifySweep helps investigate it.

You do not need to prove fraud, solve the case yourself, or know which Google policy may apply. Bring us what you found. VerifySweep helps investigate the company or listing, connect related evidence, separate verified facts from suspicion, and prepare a factual case for Google review when the evidence supports it.

What you bring

A Google Business Profile, company name, phone number, website, address, screenshots, ads, credential claim, or any other pattern that does not add up. Your report starts the investigation; it does not decide the outcome.

How VerifySweep helps investigate

1. Review the report. We separate what you personally observed from what you suspect and identify the facts that can be independently checked.

2. Check the company and listing. We review business identity, claimed locations, websites, domains, phone numbers, credential claims, and other available source material.

3. Look for connections. AI-assisted tools can surface shared phones, domains, addresses, names, wording, related listings, and prior reports that may be relevant. A connection is documented as a connection—not automatically treated as proof of wrongdoing.

4. Organize the evidence. VerifySweep separates reporter allegations, independently verified facts, unresolved questions, and supporting source material so the case can be reviewed clearly.

5. Compare verified facts with Google policy. When the evidence may relate to Business Profile eligibility, representation, fake-location, impersonation, or lead-generation concerns, VerifySweep helps identify the relevant policy issues without pretending to make Google's enforcement decision.

6. Prepare the report for Google review. If the evidence supports a policy concern, VerifySweep helps turn scattered screenshots, URLs, dates, and observations into a concise factual package for the proper Google reporting channel.

7. Human review stays in the loop. AI assists with triage, pattern-finding, evidence organization, and case preparation. It does not declare a company fraudulent or make the final judgment.

You bring the concern. VerifySweep helps investigate, organize, and prepare the evidence.
Google makes the enforcement decision. A report to VerifySweep is the beginning of an investigation—not a verdict.

DUE PROCESS

This is not a competitor complaint board.

A company does not become “fraudulent” because another sweep reported it. VerifySweep separates allegations from verified facts, records the source for each claim, and keeps human review between an automated indicator and any public-facing conclusion.

START A REPORT

Report a company or listing that deserves a closer look.

Stick to what you observed. The stronger the source material, the more useful the investigation.

Important: Do not submit a company simply because it is a competitor, has lower prices, or has negative reviews. Report conduct or representations you can describe and support.

Google currently lists lead-generation agents or companies as ineligible for a Business Profile. A lead-generation indicator is still only an indicator: document the business model and supporting evidence rather than assuming what the company is.

Evidence standard: Separate what you personally observed from what you suspect. Strong reports identify the exact listing, preserve URLs or screenshots, and explain relationships that another reviewer can independently check.
By submitting, you are asking VerifySweep to review potential indicators. You are not asking VerifySweep to automatically declare the company fraudulent or to guarantee removal from Google.

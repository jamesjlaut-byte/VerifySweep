PRO SWEEP GUIDE

Build a Google complaint around evidence—not frustration.

A strong report identifies the exact listing, documents what can be independently checked, separates suspicion from fact and uses the reporting channel that fits the issue.

Important: VerifySweep helps organize evidence and prepare a complaint. Google decides whether a listing violates policy and what enforcement action, if any, is appropriate.

Complaint workflow

Preserve the listing. Save the exact Maps/Profile URL, displayed business name, phone, website, address/service area and screenshots with dates.
Write verified observations separately. State what you personally checked. Do not convert a theory about ownership, reviews or lead routing into a fact.
Document relationships. Shared domains, phones, addresses or page content can be relevant leads. A relationship alone does not prove wrongdoing.
Match evidence to current Google policy. Use official policy language and avoid stretching a rule to fit a competitor complaint.
Choose the appropriate Google route. Misleading business information may fit Google's Business Redressal Complaint Form; other Maps or review issues have separate reporting processes.
Keep the complaint concise. Lead with the strongest independently checkable facts, then supporting sources.

Credential Stacking: What Multiple Chimney Credentials Really Mean

Multiple relevant credentials can show broader educational investment, but logos should never replace competence, judgment, and field experience.

Credentials can cover different competencies.

A foundational chimney credential, a fuel-specific hearth credential, an inspection-focused credential, manufacturer training, and continuing education may each address different parts of the work.

More is not automatically better.

VerifySweep does not award a simple score based on the number of logos. The useful questions are what the credential covers, whether it is current, who holds it, and whether it is relevant to the work being performed.

What a strong professional profile can show

A transparent profile can identify the individual credential holder, issuing organization, credential type, verification source, specialized training, and relevant experience without implying that any credential guarantees workmanship.

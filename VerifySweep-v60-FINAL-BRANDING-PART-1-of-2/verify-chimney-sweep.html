HOMEOWNER VERIFICATION GUIDE

Verify a chimney sweep before you hire.

Do not stop at a company logo, a Google listing or the words “certified chimney sweep.” VerifySweep helps you identify the individual professional, check the credential at its official source and understand who is actually coming to your home.

If you are searching Google for how to verify a chimney sweep, the most important distinction is simple: many chimney credentials belong to an individual person, not automatically to every technician working for a company. Verification should connect a real person to a current credential and a real business.

Where can I verify chimney sweep credentials?

Recognized chimney and hearth organizations maintain their own credential or professional resources. VerifySweep points homeowners back to those official sources instead of asking you to trust a badge displayed on a contractor’s website.

What does verification actually prove?

Credential verification can help confirm that a named individual appears to hold a credential issued by the organization you checked. It does not guarantee workmanship, honesty, code compliance or the outcome of a job. It is one independently checkable piece of a better hiring decision.

Red flags when trying to verify a chimney sweep

Slow down if a company will not tell you who is coming, advertises a certification without identifying the credential holder, uses vague phrases such as “fully certified” without naming the issuer, or gives business information that does not match across the website, phone number and listing.

None of those facts alone proves wrongdoing. They are reasons to verify more carefully before approving work or expensive repairs.

VerifySweep principle: Verify the person. Verify the credential. Verify the company. Then decide who you want working on your chimney.

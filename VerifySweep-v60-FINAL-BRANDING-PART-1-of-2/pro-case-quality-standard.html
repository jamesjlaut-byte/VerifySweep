PROFESSIONAL STANDARD

The VerifySweep Case Quality Standard

A useful case is traceable, reproducible, restrained, and clear about what is known versus suspected.

1. Identifiable

The case identifies the exact company, profile, URL, location, phone number, website, or other subject being reviewed.

2. Source-backed

Important factual statements should be tied to a source, screenshot, record, direct observation, or other material a reviewer can inspect.

3. Time-aware

Online information changes. Preserve dates and distinguish current observations from historical evidence.

4. Properly labeled

Reporter allegations, verified facts, possible relationships, unresolved questions, and policy analysis should not be blended together.

5. Proportionate

The conclusion should never be stronger than the evidence. VerifySweep organizes potential indicators; Google or another relevant authority makes its own enforcement decision.

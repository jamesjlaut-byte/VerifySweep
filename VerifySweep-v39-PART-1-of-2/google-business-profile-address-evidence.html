Documenting Business Profile Address Evidence

A professional guide to documenting address and location concerns without treating an address mismatch as automatic proof of a violation.

1. Start with the exact profile

Preserve the business name, Maps URL, displayed address or service-area status, category, phone and website before drawing conclusions.

2. Check the location carefully

Use reliable public sources to understand what the address represents. A residence, office, shared building or commercial suite is not automatically improper.

3. Separate access from eligibility

Record what can actually be established about signage, staffing, customer access or authority to represent the location. Do not infer facts you cannot verify.

4. Build reproducible evidence

Save dated screenshots, source URLs and a short explanation of what another reviewer can independently confirm.

5. Compare facts with current policy

Only after evidence is collected should the case be compared with Google Business Profile eligibility and representation rules.

VerifySweep standard: Evidence should support the exact statement being made. A suspicious indicator is a reason to investigate, not a verdict.
